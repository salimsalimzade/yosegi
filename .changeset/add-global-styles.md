Add design tokens, light and dark themes, fonts and global styles. Import `yosegi/styles.css` once at your app's entry, or `yosegi/tokens.css` for the tokens alone. Every token is a `--yosegi-*` custom property you can override in your own CSS, and `data-yosegi-theme` forces a theme. Red Hat Mono and Red Hat Text are self-hosted through `yosegi/fonts.css`.
